Delete your account

HabitSpy · Habit & sobriety tracker

HabitSpy works without an account. If you signed in with Apple or Google to back up and sync your data, you can permanently delete that account and all of its data at any time.

Option 1 — In the app (instant)

  1. Open HabitSpy and tap the gear icon to open Settings.
  2. Under Data, tap Delete account (shown while you're signed in) and confirm.

Your account and all its synced data are deleted immediately.

Option 2 — By email (without the app)

If you no longer have the app installed, email us and tell us the email address of the Apple or Google account you used to sign in:

📧 habitspy.app@gmail.com
Subject: Delete my HabitSpy account.

To protect your data, we never delete an account just because someone asked. We send a confirmation email to the address linked to the account and delete it only after a reply from that address. Your account and its data are deleted within 30 days of your confirmation.

Used Apple's "Hide My Email"? Tell us the relay address instead (it looks like …@privaterelay.appleid.com). On your iPhone, find it in Settings → your name → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple → HabitSpy. Our confirmation reaches you through Apple's relay.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (sign-in identifier, and name and email if you shared them)
  • All synced habits and daily logs
  • Feedback messages you sent while signed in
  • For Sign in with Apple: the stored Apple sign-in token

What isn't covered

  • Subscriptions are not cancelled by deleting your account. Cancel Premium in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions or in the App Store → your account → Subscriptions.
  • Data on your phone that was never synced stays on the device until you uninstall the app or reset it.
  • Purchase records are kept by Google, Apple and our subscription provider RevenueCat as required for billing and tax.
  • Anonymous crash reports and feedback sent without an account contain no personal identifiers and can't be linked to you.
