Delete your account

HabitSpy · Habit & sobriety tracker

HabitSpy works without an account. If you signed in with Apple or Google to back up and sync your data, you can permanently delete that account and all of its data at any time.

Option 1 — In the app (instant)

  1. Open HabitSpy and tap the gear icon to open Settings.
  2. Under Data, tap Delete account (shown while you're signed in) and confirm.

Your account and all its synced data are deleted immediately.
