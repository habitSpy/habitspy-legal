Common questions

Backing up & syncing my data

HabitSpy works fully without an account and keeps your data on your device. To back it up and sync across devices, sign in with Google (or Apple, on iPhone) in Settings. Your habits then sync privately to your account.

Managing or cancelling my subscription

Premium is an auto-renewable subscription billed by the store you bought it from. Manage or cancel it any time:

  • iPhone: App Store app → your account → Subscriptions
  • Android: Google Play app → your profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions

Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep Premium until the current period ends.

Restoring a purchase

If Premium doesn't appear after reinstalling or switching devices, open the paywall in the app and choose Restore, signed in with the same Apple ID (iPhone) or Google account (Android) that made the purchase.

Deleting my account & data

You can permanently delete your account and all its synced data at any time from Settings → Delete account inside the app. No longer have the app? See how to request deletion by email.

Reminders aren't showing

Make sure notifications are allowed for HabitSpy — on iPhone in Settings → HabitSpy → Notifications, on Android in Settings → Apps → HabitSpy → Notifications — then re-enable the daily reminder in the app's Settings. Some Android phones (for example Xiaomi or Samsung) delay notifications to save battery; if reminders arrive late or not at all, turn off battery optimization for HabitSpy in the phone's battery settings.
