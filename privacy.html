Privacy Policy

HabitSpy · Last updated 27 September 2026

habitSpy ("the app", "we", "us") is a habit-tracking app operated by Yuriy Protsyk, an individual developer based in Ukraine. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and your choices. habitSpy is local-first and anonymous-first: you can use it fully without an account, and your tracking data stays on your device unless you choose to sign in.

This document describes the app's data practices, including features that may not be active in every version (for example cloud sync, subscriptions, analytics, or ads are only in effect once you use or enable them).

1. Data stored on your device

Your habits and daily logs are stored locally on your device in the app's private storage. If you never sign in, this data never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the app removes it.

2. Account data (only if you sign in)

Signing in is optional and exists to back up and sync your data. We offer Sign in with Apple and Sign in with Google. When you sign in we receive, via our backend provider Supabase, a user identifier and — where you permit it — your name and email address. Apple's Hide My Email and similar options are respected. We use this only to identify your account and sync your data.

3. Synced tracking data (only if you sign in)

When signed in, your habits and daily logs are backed up to our backend (Supabase, hosted in the EU). This data is transmitted over encrypted connections and protected by access rules so that only your account can read or write it. Because habit data can reveal sensitive information (for example alcohol consumption), we treat it with care, never sell it, and never use it for advertising.

4. Feedback

If you send feedback from within the app, we store the message you write along with your app version and device platform (for example "iOS"), to help us fix issues and improve the app.

5. Purchases

Premium subscriptions are sold through the Apple App Store and managed with RevenueCat. We and RevenueCat receive your subscription status and a purchase identifier so we can unlock premium features. We never receive your card or payment details — those are handled entirely by Apple.

6. Diagnostics & crash reports

To find and fix problems, the app may send crash reports and technical diagnostics (for example error messages, stack traces, device model, OS version, app version) to Sentry. These reports are configured to exclude your habit content and personal identifiers; they describe what went wrong, not what you tracked.

7. Analytics

The app may use privacy-conscious product analytics (PostHog) to understand how features are used — for example which screens are opened or which buttons are tapped — in an aggregated, behavioural way. Analytics never include your habit names or logged data.

8. Advertising (free tier)

The free version may show ads via Google AdMob, which may use device/advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads. AdMob may also infer your approximate, city-level location from your IP address for the same purpose; this is coarse location only, not precise GPS. Premium removes ads. See Google's policies at policies.google.com/technologies/ads. On iOS, ad tracking is subject to Apple's App Tracking Transparency and your choice.

9. Notifications

Optional daily reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Enabling them does not send any data to us.

10. Third parties we rely on

  • Apple — sign-in, payments, app distribution
  • Google — sign-in, and AdMob advertising (free tier)
  • Supabase — authentication and encrypted data sync (EU-hosted)
  • RevenueCat — subscription management
  • Sentry — crash and error diagnostics
  • PostHog — aggregated product analytics

Each processes data under its own privacy terms. We do not sell your personal data to anyone.

11. Retention & deletion

Local data lives until you delete it in the app or uninstall. Synced data is kept while your account exists. Signing out removes the synced copy from that device (it remains in your account for your other devices). Deleting your account removes your synced data from our backend. To request deletion, use the in-app option or contact us below.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including the EU/EEA under the GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to certain processing. Contact us to exercise these rights.

13. Children

habitSpy is intended for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

14. Security

We use reputable providers and encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

15. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected here with a new "Last updated" date.
